---
name: Focused navigation
description: Navigation hierarchy for the architectural drawing register
---

The app should feel like one focused drawing-register workspace rather than a collection of equally weighted modules. Keep Overview, Drawing register, Projects, Assignments, Review Queue, Deadlines, and collaboration visible as the daily path; keep reference, reporting, administration, settings, and archival tools in one discoverable secondary menu.

**Why:** The product contains many legitimate capabilities, but showing every route with equal weight makes the app feel repetitive and harder to scan.

**How to apply:** Preserve all routes and search access when simplifying navigation. Prefer contextual links on the current page and avoid adding duplicate dashboard shortcuts for destinations already present in the primary shell.
